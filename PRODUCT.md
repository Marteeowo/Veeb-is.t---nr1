# Broneeringud

## Register

product

## Purpose and users

Estonian school assignment: a teacher-inspectable, read-only booking dashboard demonstrating Fetch API, DOM manipulation and service-specific CSS classes. The supplied assignment is the authoritative product brief.

## Design direction

Confident, calm, clear. A compact salon appointment desk with a charcoal masthead, honey accent, white working surface and four muted service colors. Avoid a basic unstyled school table, decorative fake controls, saturated row fills and unnecessary frameworks.

## Principles

- All records come from JSON; no booking rows in the initial HTML.
- Search and service filters work together; statistics describe the complete dataset.
- Be honest about sample dates and source availability.
- Support keyboard use, visible focus, reduced motion and narrow screens.
- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript; no build step or runtime dependencies.
